What you're looking at
  • The bubble shows how many things are due, and pulses while a session with this client is on now. With nothing due it goes quiet (try "Everything up to date").
  • The score is the Trainer Portal's own Client Health Score, with the portal's colours. No portal score = "No score yet" and the reason.
  • Up to 5 lines holding the score back, then this session's lines. A Kickstart client's third session books the strategy session; their other sessions get them onto their first solo workout.
  • Ticked lines sink to the bottom. A tick is a note for today; it never changes the score.
